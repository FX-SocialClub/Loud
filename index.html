THE CUSTOMER

You call in as a customer, get his real numbers out of him, then flip to the offer with his own math in your hand. English is the base — the RU button under every block is your safety net.

Before you dial

Block 1 · you are a customer, nothing else

📞 OPENING

01 / 11
📞 OWNER PICKED UP
From this second you are Cane, a guy who needs his apartment cleaned. Nothing about websites until the trigger question.
I SAY · opening as a customer
"Hi, my name is Cane, I'm looking for a cleaning service. It's a 2-bedroom apartment, about 800 square feet, downtown. Just standard recurring cleaning — should be about 4 hours. How much would that cost?"
Почему 800, а не 80: 80 кв. футов — это кладовка. Владелец ловит фальшь мгновенно, и звонок мёртв. 800 кв. футов = нормальная двушка.
↓
← "I'd have to see the place"
CLIENT SAYS
"I can't quote without seeing it."
↓
I SAY · push for a range
"Got it — I'll send you photos on WhatsApp. But give me a range right now, just so I know the budget. It's a 4-hour job."
→ he names a number
I SAY · lock the number in
"So for 800 square feet we're looking at around $300–500? Okay, bro, got it."
↓
🎯 ACTION
Write the number down. Everything in the money block is built on it — not on a made-up story.
Block 2 · one at a time, casual, still just a customer

🔍 DISCOVERY

02 / 11
QUESTION 1 · how booked they are
"Could we book it for the same day, or are you booked out for the week?"
QUESTION 2 · years + decision maker
"Sounds good. How long have you been doing this? …And you're the owner, right?"
Ключевой момент: если он не ЛПР — переходи к возражению «I'm not the owner» в листе возражений.
QUESTION 3 · capacity
"It's not a secret — how many jobs can you fit in a day?"
QUESTION 4 · solo or crew
"Do you run the jobs yourself, or do you have a crew?"
QUESTION 5 · traffic source
"Okay, bro — tell me one more thing. Where do your customers come from right now? Yelp, Google, Facebook, referrals?"
Why one at a time

Пять вопросов подряд = допрос, и он закрывается. Один вопрос — его ответ — твоя реакция («okay», «sounds good») — следующий вопрос. Ты клиент, который просто болтает, а не менеджер, заполняющий анкету. К концу блока у тебя есть: цена за объект, объектов в день, стаж, ЛПР, канал трафика.

03 / 11

Пауза. Дай ответить до конца. Не заполняй тишину.

↓

He just confirmed the channel himself. Now you have a live thread to send the demo into — that was the whole point of the call.
Block 4 · real competitors beat an invented story

🏙 COMPARISON

04 / 11
I SAY · competitors by name
"Before I called I looked at a few of your competitors in town. They've got a serious website — AI integration, the whole thing. Looks like a $3,000 site. And for the same 800 square-foot job they charge $400–700. They've been around less time than you and they have less experience. But they get the first impression, because the website already looks premium."
Обязательно: называй реальные имена, которые нашёл в prep. «Один парень в вашем городе» — это пустой звук, а «[Name] Cleaning на первой странице Google» — это факт, который он может проверить и который его цепляет.
Block 5 · his numbers, not yours

💰 MONEY MATH

05 / 11
Extra per month
× 22 working days
$2,640
$400 → $440 per job · 3 jobs/day · 22 days = +$2,640/mo + a premium brand image
I SAY · say it slow
"Now picture that over a month, at your volume — an extra $20, $30, $50 on every single job."
⚠ How to phrase the numbers

"Out of 10 inquiries you'll close 5 instead of 2" — say it as example math, not as a promise: "in our cases we've seen conversion roughly double." An owner who counts money spots an empty guarantee in a second — and that's exactly where you lose the deal. Real competitors plus his own numbers convince far harder than an invented story.

Block 6 · four parts, in this order

📢 THE OFFER

06 / 11
PART 1 · what it is
"We build modern websites where the whole build runs on AI. I'm talking about a site that looks like $5,000 — and three years ago that's exactly what it cost. Today, with AI tools, it's 2–3 days of work and the price of 3 of your jobs."
PART 2 · the GPT chatbot
"The site has a GPT-powered chatbot built in. It walks the customer through it and builds the request — what, where, how big, when. It checks your Google Calendar and offers the slots you actually have open."
PART 3 · the request hits your phone
"The request lands on your phone: Friday 5 PM, this address, 800 square feet, deep clean. You approve it — the bot locks the customer in and blocks 5 to 8 PM on your calendar."
PART 4 · Google & support
"It gets indexed on Google, so it brings you customers without ad spend. We do everything turnkey, hosting and upkeep included. All we need from you is the business name and what you want. After launch we stay on for edits."

"Perfect — is this number good for a text, or do you prefer WhatsApp? You'll have it within the hour. Thanks, have a good one!"

🏆 GOAL ACHIEVED
Text him within the hour, while he still remembers the call. Don't "sleep on it" — by tomorrow you're a stranger again.

Sheet 2 · objection handling

🛡 OBJECTIONS

10 / 11
🎯 REMEMBER
You are not selling a website on this call. You are selling 30 seconds of looking at a link. Every answer below ends by pointing back at that.
❌ BASE ANSWER TO "NOT INTERESTED"
"All I'm doing is showing you what your competitors' sites look like — zero obligation. One minute, and if it doesn't land, you just say thanks."
❌ "How much does it cost?"
"Depends on scope — starts at $__. But look at the visual first: if it doesn't grab you, the price is irrelevant anyway. That's why I want to show you before we talk money."
❌ "Why so cheap? What's the catch?"
"No catch — AI does the heavy lifting, so the cost dropped by an order of magnitude. This price is for the first clients in your niche; it goes up after that. You see the result first, then you decide."
❌ "Yelp / Facebook is enough for me"
"Facebook works for people who already know you. A website catches the ones Googling 'house cleaning [city]' right now who've never heard of you."
❌ "I'm booked solid, I don't need more customers"
"Then it's not a customer problem — it's a pricing problem. A site is the reason to raise your rate 15–20% and make more on the exact same schedule."
❌ "No time, I'm on a job"
"That's exactly why I'm suggesting a text — look at it whenever you get a free minute. Is tonight fine?"
❌ "I've got a guy who does websites"
"Fair enough, let him build it. Look at mine for comparison — then you'll know for sure what you're choosing. One minute."
❌ "Where did you get my number?"
"From your own listing — you publish it so customers can call. That's exactly how I called, as a customer."
❌ "I'm not the owner"
"Got it. Who handles the growth side over there? What's his name, and when's a good time to reach him?"
❌ "Just email me something"
"I'll do both — but honestly, emails get buried. Let me text the link too, it opens straight in your phone browser. What's the best email?"
↓ after any objection → re-close
🔄 RE-CLOSE
"You agreed yourself that a website is image and trust. And you're a business owner, you run the numbers: a site for the price of 2–3 jobs that looks like it cost thousands. The prices I quoted are for the first clients in your area — they go up after that. So let me text you, and I want to hear what you think."

"I hear that a lot. Usually the issue is the site looked okay but wasn't built to convert visitors into calls. That's exactly what we fix. And you see the result before paying anything."

🚨 Hard situations — rare, but you need the answer ready
❌ "Where did you get my number?"
"Totally fair question. I found your business online while researching local [industry] businesses in the area. I'm not working off any list — I just came across [Business] and had an idea."
❌ "Are you local?"
"We work specifically with businesses in California. We know the local market well and most of our clients are in [City / area]."
❌ "Who else do you work with?"
"We work with local service businesses — restaurants, contractors, salons, auto shops — across California. I'd love to show you examples on our quick call."
❌ "Can you guarantee results?"
"Here's what I can guarantee — you'll see the finished website before paying a dollar. If it doesn't look better than what you have now, you don't pay. That's our model."
❌ "We already do SEO / promotion"
"That's great — SEO drives traffic, but if the site doesn't convert that traffic into calls, you're paying for visitors that never become customers. We fix the conversion side."
❌ "We don't spend on marketing"
"I get that. This isn't really marketing — it's your storefront online. Every customer who hears about you looks you up. Right now what they find either helps you or hurts you."
❌ "Call me next month"
"Of course. Can I ask — is there something specific happening next month that makes it a better time? I want to make sure I call at the right moment for you."
↓
🎯 ACTION
Write the date → set a reminder → call back exactly on time.
❌ "We're fully booked"
"That's the best problem to have. Quick question — is it consistent year-round, or are there slower months? And are you getting the kind of clients you actually want, or a mixed bag?"

"Look — can I just send you the demo? No calls unless you want. Just so you have it." → then follow up in 7 days.
